Silverpoint

A free RAW camera app for Android, made for the Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra. It skips Samsung's image processing and develops every photo on the GPU with one of 21 film-inspired looks.

Download the APK Source code on GitHub

Beta · Android 14+ · open source (Apache 2.0) · no internet permission

What it does

  • RAW on all four rear lenses0.6×, 1×, 3× and 5×, straight from the sensor, without Samsung's multi-frame processing.
  • 21 film-inspired looksEach built from a Lightroom preset: curves, colour grading, clarity, vignette and film grain.
  • Live previewsSee your scene through every look at once, about 30 times a second, and star your favourites.
  • Pro modeDials with haptic detents for exposure, ISO, shutter speed, focus and Kelvin white balance.
  • Cleaner low lightSix frames aligned and merged, with moving objects left out.
  • Ultra HDR JPEGsHighlights glow on HDR screens; photos go straight to your Gallery, with an optional DNG.
  • Private by designNo internet permission, no accounts, no analytics, no location in your photos.
  • Four shapes3:4, 4:5, 1:1 and 9:16; the DNG always keeps the whole sensor.

Install

  1. On your Galaxy S25 Ultra, download Silverpoint.apk (older versions are on the releases page).
  2. Open it and allow installs from your browser or file manager when Android asks.
  3. Open Silverpoint and allow camera access.

Silverpoint is tested on the Galaxy S25 Ultra. Other phones with RAW support may work; its Diagnostics screen tells you. See the README for details and known limitations.
