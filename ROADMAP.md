# Roadmap

What comes after the first release. The README's [Upcoming features](README.md#upcoming-features) section is the short version of this list.

## Next update

- [ ] **Import your own Lightroom presets:** `.xmp` files, DNG "mobile presets" and `.zip` packs, shown in a "My looks" tab. The app lists any settings it had to ignore. Feasibility study: [docs/proposals/XMP_IMPORT.md](docs/proposals/XMP_IMPORT.md).
- [ ] **Re-develop a past photo with another look:** open a shot taken with its DNG and develop it again with a different look.
- [ ] **Look strength:** blend any look from 0 to 100% with the neutral rendering, like Lightroom's preset Amount.
- [ ] **Film effects:** halation (the glow around bright lights), light leaks, film borders and an optional date stamp.
- [ ] **Volume-key shutter and AE/AF lock:** a volume key takes the photo, and a long press on the viewfinder locks exposure and focus.
- [ ] **Before/after compare:** hold a photo's thumbnail to compare the look with the neutral rendering.
- [ ] **More Galaxy phones, added gradually:** each phone is enabled only after proper testing. To help, open a *Device report* issue with:
  - the Diagnostics report (Settings → Diagnostics);
  - one JPEG taken with Silverpoint;
  - the DNG of the same shot (Settings → Also save the DNG).

## Planned

- [ ] **Make your own look:** an editor built from the engine's sliders (tone, curve, colour mixer, grain, vignette).
- [ ] **Long exposure:** stacked frames added together for real motion blur beyond the phone's ~1/6 s limit.
- [ ] **Exposure bracketing:** 3–5 shots at different exposures in one press.
- [ ] **Intervalometer and time-lapse.**
- [ ] **Landscape layout:** controls that rotate with the phone (needed for foldables on Android 16).
- [ ] **Open from the side key and other apps:** register as a camera app.
- [ ] **Quick Settings tile and home-screen shortcut** to open straight to a chosen look.
- [ ] **Opt-in location tagging**, off by default.
