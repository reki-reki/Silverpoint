# Looks

Silverpoint ships 21 looks in [`app/src/main/assets/recipes.json`](../app/src/main/assets/recipes.json). Each one has a matching Lightroom preset in [`docs/reference/presets/`](reference/presets/) with the same parameters, which is used to tune and compare the app's output.

**Names.** The looks have original names; the app shows no film or camera brand names. This document is the only place that says what inspired each one. That is done to describe it, not to claim any link: the looks are original approximations of each reference's *character*, matched by eye and against Lightroom. They are not Fujifilm's or Kodak's colour science, and not copies of any camera's in-camera settings. Product names below are trademarks of their owners (see the [README](../README.md#trademarks-and-disclaimer)).

**Sets.**
- `core`: the first seven.
- `popular`: the other fourteen.
- **Film stocks tab:** the app also shows a separate tab for looks in the `Film` group.

**Credit.** Recipe popularity and character references come from the [Fuji X Weekly](https://fujixweekly.com/) community; *Reference* names the page each look is based on.

**Licence.** The presets are licensed with the rest of the repository under Apache-2.0.
